Services

Colour is our speciality, from a subtle balayage to a full correction. Every visit starts with a short consultation.

About prices. Colour prices depend on your hair's length, thickness and the result you want. Send a photo on WhatsApp and we'll give you a clear quote before you book. Ask about our colour, styling and treatment package too.

Colour & balayage

Hand-painted colour that grows out softly. We choose the shade with you, in daylight, against your skin tone.

  • Balayage & ombréSoft, natural sweeps of light, including subtle balayage on dark hair.
  • Highlights & lowlightsFine or bold, foiled for dimension.
  • Root melt & toningBlends regrowth into the lengths so colour lasts between visits.
  • Grey blendingSoftens grey without a hard line of regrowth.
  • Blonding & platinumFrom warm honey to icy platinum, lifted gently.
  • Vivid colourCopper, red, burgundy, rose and violet.

Colour correction

Uneven colour, banding or brassiness from a past appointment. We plan the fix with you and protect the hair as we go, sometimes over more than one visit.

  • Consultation firstWe look at your hair's history and tell you honestly what is possible.
  • Bond-protected liftingLightening done in steps to keep strength and shine.
  • Tone & finishA gloss and blow-dry so you leave with the full result.
