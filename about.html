The salon

Ivy Beauty & Bubbles at the Sheraton Grand is a hair colour salon wrapped in living greenery, a few steps from Dubai World Trade Centre.

How we work

We listen first. Every colour starts with a conversation about what you want, what your hair has been through, and what will still look good as it grows.

We tell you honestly when a big change needs more than one visit, and we protect the hair at every step. Guests often say they came to us after a colour went wrong elsewhere.

The space follows the name: an ivy wall, a carved stone face, white marble, warm wood and arched mirrors framed in gold.
